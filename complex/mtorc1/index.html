Oliver's mTOR Atlas · Topics · mTORC1

mTORC1

Pathway/Complex · 177 studies in the Atlas · also known as TORC1, mTOR complex 1, mTOR complex-1

mTOR Complex 1; regulates protein synthesis, autophagy, and growth in response to nutrients and growth factors.

The growth decision itself.

Coincidence detector: nutrients supply location via the Rags, growth factors supply activation via Rheb. Neither alone is sufficient – the central idea of the amino-acid route.

TL;DR. mTORC1 appears in 177 curated studies in Oliver's mTOR Atlas: 14 in people (14 human studies); 45 in animal models; 89 at the molecular level (cells, biochemistry, structure); 26 reviews; 3 preprints, not peer-reviewed. It has 39 interactions in the curated pathway model.
