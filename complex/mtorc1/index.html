mTORC1

Pathway/Complex · 75 studies in the Atlas · also known as TORC1, mTOR complex 1, mTOR complex-1

mTOR Complex 1; regulates protein synthesis, autophagy, and growth in response to nutrients and growth factors.

The growth decision itself.

Coincidence detector: nutrients supply location via the Rags, growth factors supply activation via Rheb. Neither alone is sufficient — the single most important idea in the pathway.
