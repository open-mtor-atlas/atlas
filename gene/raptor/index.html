Raptor

Gene/Protein · 7 studies in the Atlas · also known as raptor, RPTOR, KOG1

Defining subunit of mTORC1; scaffold that presents substrates (S6K1, 4E-BP1) to mTOR. Its presence is what makes a complex 'mTORC1'.
