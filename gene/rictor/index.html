Rictor

Gene/Protein · 3 studies in the Atlas · also known as rictor, RICTOR, AVO3

Defining subunit of mTORC2 (Rapamycin-Insensitive Companion of mTOR); routes mTOR toward Akt and the cytoskeleton rather than S6K1/4E-BP1.
