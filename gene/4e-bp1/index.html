4E-BP1

Gene/Protein · 7 studies in the Atlas · also known as 4E-BP, 4EBP1, EIF4EBP1, PHAS-I

Translational repressor; the master effector through which mTORC1 controls protein synthesis. mTORC1 phosphorylates 4E-BP1 to release eIF4E and switch translation ON.
