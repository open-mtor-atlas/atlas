TSC1/TSC2

Gene/Protein · 9 studies in the Atlas · also known as TSC1, TSC2, TSC1-TSC2, TSC complex, hamartin, tuberin

Tuberin-hamartin tumor suppressor complex; acts as a GTPase-activating protein for Rheb; integrates growth-factor and energy signals to control mTORC1.
