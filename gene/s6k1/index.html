S6K1

Gene/Protein · 8 studies in the Atlas · also known as p70 S6K, p70 S6 kinase, S6 kinase 1, RPS6KB1, p70S6K

Direct downstream effector of mTORC1 controlling protein synthesis; its deletion extends mouse lifespan similarly to caloric restriction.
