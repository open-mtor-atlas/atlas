**Title:** mTOR Academy — Oliver's mTOR Atlas

**URL:** https://mtor-atlas.org/academy/core/

**Material Type:** Full Course, Lesson

**Description:**
A free, 10-lesson self-paced course on the mechanistic biology of mTOR
(mechanistic target of rapamycin) signaling, built inside a larger
evidence-graded research database of 354 curated primary studies. Each
lesson answers one question (e.g. "What is mTOR?", "mTORC1 vs mTORC2 —
what's the difference?") and links every mechanistic claim to the
primary study behind it, tagged by evidence tier (A = systematic
review/meta-analysis through D = mechanistic/in-vitro). Includes
think-questions, a short quiz per lesson, and a closing lesson on how to
critically read a primary mTOR paper. Free, no account required, CC BY
4.0, self-paced/asynchronous.

**Subject:** Science → Biology → Molecular Biology; Science → Biology →
Cell Biology

**Education Level:** Higher Education, Community College/Lower Division,
Career/Technical (advanced high school and general-public learners are
also well served, if that option exists on the current form)

**License:** CC BY 4.0

**Language:** English

**Author(s):** Oliver Barton (ORCID: 0009-0008-2025-2148)

**Keywords/Tags:** mTOR, mTORC1, mTORC2, signal transduction, autophagy,
rapamycin, aging biology, cell signaling, molecular biology,
evidence-based, open educational resource

**Accessibility notes (if the form asks):** Text-based HTML lessons, no
required plugins, works without JavaScript for reading (interactive
quiz/exercise elements are progressive enhancement, not required to read
the lesson content).
