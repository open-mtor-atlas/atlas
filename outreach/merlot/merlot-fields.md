**Material Title:** mTOR Academy — Oliver's mTOR Atlas

**URL:** https://mtor-atlas.org/academy/core/

**Description:**
A free, 10-lesson self-paced course teaching the mechanistic biology of
mTOR (mechanistic target of rapamycin) signaling — from "what is mTOR"
through nutrient sensing, growth factor signaling, feedback regulation,
and autophagy — for students and self-directed learners with a basic
biology background. Every mechanistic claim links to the primary study
behind it in Oliver's mTOR Atlas, an evidence-graded database of 354
curated mTOR studies, with that study's evidence tier (A–D) attached.
Each lesson includes think-questions and a short quiz; the course closes
with a lesson on how to critically read a primary mTOR paper. Free, no
account required, CC BY 4.0.

**Material Type:** Tutorial

**Technical Format:** HTML

**Cost Involved:** No

**Source Code Available:** No (content, not software)

**Author(s):** Oliver Barton (ORCID: 0009-0008-2025-2148)

**Language:** English

**Primary Audience:** Higher Education — Undergraduate Upper Division,
Undergraduate Lower Division; also suitable for advanced high school and
general adult learners

**Category/Discipline:** Biology → Molecular & Cell Biology; also
relevant to Biology → Genetics

**Keywords:** mTOR, mTORC1, mTORC2, signal transduction, cell signaling,
autophagy, rapamycin, aging biology, molecular biology, kinase signaling

**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

**Additional notes for the reviewer (not a form field, context only):**
This course sits inside a larger evidence-graded research database
(mtor-atlas.org) rather than standing alone — the "Learn the biology"
links from individual study pages point back into these lessons, and
each lesson's "Related studies" section points out to the primary
literature. Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22059963.
