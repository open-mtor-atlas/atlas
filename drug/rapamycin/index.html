Rapamycin

Drug · 36 studies in the Atlas · also known as Sirolimus, RAPA, AY-22989

Sirolimus; mTORC1 inhibitor (and, with chronic dosing, mTORC2 too); immunosuppressant; extends lifespan in mice.
