Abstract

Gambogic acid (GA), a natural xanthonoid compound, was investigated for its anticancer activity in lung cancer via mTOR signaling inhibition using computational and in vitro approaches. GA demonstrated high lipophilicity and docking interactions with mTOR pathway components, resulting in anti-proliferative and pro-apoptotic effects in lung cancer cell lines.
