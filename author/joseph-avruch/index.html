Joseph Avruch

Co-discovered raptor and showed Rheb directly activates mTOR · MD, Washington University in St. Louis · Professor of Medicine, Harvard Medical School · Chief, Diabetes Unit, Massachusetts General Hospital (since 1979)

Joseph Avruch has led the Diabetes Unit at Massachusetts General Hospital since 1979, building a career tracing how insulin and growth-factor signalling control protein synthesis and cell growth — work that converged on mTOR as insulin's downstream growth-control checkpoint.

His lab independently identified raptor as mTOR's nutrient-sensitive binding partner in 2002, and in 2005 showed that the small GTPase Rheb binds directly to mTOR's kinase domain and activates it — establishing Rheb as the molecular switch that couples growth-factor signalling, through the TSC1/TSC2 complex, to mTORC1 activity.
