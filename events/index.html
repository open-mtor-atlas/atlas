What Tier 1–3 mean
Tier 1
mTOR is the subject. A named mTOR session, a disease whose standard of care is mTOR inhibition, or an Atlas top author speaking on the pathway itself. 5 meetings.
Tier 2
A core mechanism of the pathway is the subject. Autophagy, the lysosome and nutrient sensing, or rapamycin in geroscience — mTOR is not on the poster, but it is what the sessions are about. 30 meetings.
Tier 3
Adjacent field. mTOR is not a named topic; the relevance comes through overlapping biology or through individual speakers whose work the Atlas holds. 6 meetings.

The rating is Oliver's editorial judgement about the meeting's distance from this Atlas's subject. It says nothing about the quality of the science — a Tier 3 conference can be the better meeting.

26–30 Oct 2027 AACR-NCI-EORTC International Conference on Molecular Targets and Cancer Therapeutics 2027 Chicago, IL, USA Tier 2

Small, drug-development-focused conference jointly organised by AACR, the NCI and the EORTC; the main venue where first-in-human data on novel targeted agents are reported before journal publication.

Why it matters hereDirect provenance for the Atlas's own Schram 2025 entry: the first-in-human phase 1/1b data for RMC-5552, the first bi-steric mTORC1-selective inhibitor in the clinic, were presented here (Abstract C020, 2023) before the Clinical Cancer Research paper. This is where Open Question 2 (inhibit mTORC1 without affecting mTORC2) is being answered in humans rather than in mice: bi-steric selectivity relieves the mTORC2-mediated hyperglycaemia that limited earlier pan-mTOR inhibitors. Also the venue for 4E-BP1 pharmacodynamics and mucositis management.
