mTOR Academy · Beyond the Atlas

Free courses and lectures

The Academy teaches one thing, the mTOR pathway, from the studies in this Atlas. The free courses and talks below cover the ground around it: the cell biology it assumes, the signalling above it and the biology of ageing it feeds into. None of them is ours. Each one is here because it teaches something a lesson in the Academy leans on.

Links checked 28 September 2026

There is no big online course about mTOR alone. mTOR turns up as a chapter inside courses on signalling, metabolism and ageing, so every entry says how much of it you will actually get.

Where to start

New to biology

Four steps, about a week of evenings before the course itself.

  1. Michael Hall's talk, The Story of TOR
  2. David Sabatini's three-minute video
  3. MITx 7.00x or the KAIST signalling course
  4. Lesson 01 here in the Academy

Already studying biology

Read and watch these next to the lessons they match.

  1. The LibreTexts chapter, next to lessons 02, 06 and 07
  2. Sabatini's de Duve Lecture, with lesson 05
  3. Michael Hall's 85-minute Nobel Fest lecture
  4. MITx 7.06.1x, for the cell biology in depth

About mTOR itself

2

Start here if you want the pathway itself, told by the people who found it.

Lecture
Michael Hall · iBiology

The Story of TOR (Target of Rapamycin)

Michael Hall found TOR in yeast in 1991. He tells the story from a soil sample taken on Easter Island to the two protein complexes that control how big cells grow. English subtitles, and the video can be downloaded.

Textbook chapter
LibreTexts · Fundamentals of Biochemistry

mTOR and Nutrient Signaling

A free university textbook chapter: the two complexes, how rapamycin binds, how leucine reaches mTORC1 through sestrin and GATOR, and how energy (AMPK) and insulin (Akt and TSC2) feed in. Reads well next to lessons 02, 06 and 07.
