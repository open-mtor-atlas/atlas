Alzheimer's disease

Disease · 3 studies in the Atlas

Neurodegenerative disease marked by amyloid-beta plaques and tau tangles. mTOR overactivation blocks the autophagy that would clear these toxic proteins; rapamycin restores clearance in mouse models.
