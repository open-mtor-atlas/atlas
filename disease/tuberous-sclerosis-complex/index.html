Tuberous sclerosis complex

Disease · 3 studies in the Atlas

Genetic disorder from TSC1/TSC2 loss causing constitutive mTOR activation and benign tumors in brain, kidney, and elsewhere. Everolimus shrinks these tumors (EXIST trials) - the clearest proof that blocking mTOR helps a human disease.
